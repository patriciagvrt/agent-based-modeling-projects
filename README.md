# Agent-Based Modeling Projects

A collection of agent-based models developed during the MSc in
Computational Social Science at Linköping University.

The projects explore how macro-level patterns emerge from local
agent interactions across economic, social-network, and ecological systems.

## Projects

### 1. Economic Inequality and Redistribution

A simple economy simulation comparing unrestricted random monetary
exchange with a redistribution mechanism targeting economically
vulnerable agents.

**Concepts:** inequality, redistribution, emergence, stochastic simulation

**Technology:** NetLogo

---

### 2. Network Diffusion and Social Blacklisting

A network-based diffusion model comparing contagion across small-world,
scale-free and random networks.

The extended model introduces probabilistic adoption and social
blacklisting, where rejected senders may become excluded from future
interactions.

**Concepts:** diffusion, complex contagion, network structure,
social influence, information propagation

**Technology:** NetLogo, Network Extension

---

### 3. Forest Fire Tipping Point

A spatial agent-based model studying the interaction between human-caused
fire ignition, spreading fire and firefighter response.

The model explores whether a critical firefighter-to-smoker ratio exists
that prevents large-scale forest collapse.

**Concepts:** tipping points, emergence, adaptation, stochasticity,
spatial interaction

**Technology:** NetLogo
